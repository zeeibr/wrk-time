# Almanac — the one page

**What it is.** A home strength app for one person, with one coach. Four
tabs: **Today** (the day), **Moves** (the library), **Season** (the record),
**Signals** (sleep, recovery, weight, walks).

## The coach
- One document, `COACH-BRIEF.md`, is the whole of the AI's opinion. The
  planner, the move reviewer and the chat all read it. Edit it to change
  the coach; nothing is argued with in a prompt.
- Model: `claude-opus-5`, your key, your spend. Each planner week, review
  or chat message is one request; chat is about a cent.
- **The coach proposes; the app applies.** Every change is a card you tap.

## A week
- Five sessions: **sets · on the minute · sets · intervals · sets**.
- A clean week steps on for free (no request). The coach is asked when
  something changed, and every fourth week.
- Every session: flow warm-up → one bell, standing (hinge, squat or lunge,
  row, press or push) → the floor to close. At most two implements; the
  timer adds setup time when you fetch or go down.

## Three ways to work
- **Sets** — 3 × 8–12, a couple short of failure, 3-1-1 tempo. A set is
  open until you tap Done. Rest is the move's own (about 75 / 60 / 45 s).
  The only mode that can earn a step-up.
- **On the minute** — 3–5 crisp reps in the first 25 s, rest the rest.
- **Intervals** — 40/20 (30/30 with a bell over 13 lb). Conditioning.

## Loads
- Step up when **every set hits 12, twice running, in a sets session**.
  Offered, never applied. Big jumps get a "harder at this load" bridge.
- **The 35 is for the deadlift only**, once the 18 clears that gate: 3 × 5
  dead-stop, twice a week at most. The app refuses it elsewhere.
- Loads per move are yours to change from the library.

## Tests (extras, never a session)
- **Baseline** — six moves, one set each, after a session or on a rest day;
  every four weeks. Sets the working load per pattern.
- **Weekly check** — two of the six, after a session only.

## What earns what
- A **mark** = a finished planned session. Extras, tests and your own
  routines are recorded as volume and earn no mark.
- Finished sessions are frozen records; pending ones track your loads.

## Her rules, kept
- Refusals are final and cover variants ("push-up" bars the incline).
- Saved routines are never changed by the app.
- No nutrition, no fasting, no medical advice. Pain that is sharp, in a
  joint, or still there next morning ends the set.
- Whoop is read only; "Copy for Whoop" puts the session on the clipboard.

## Good to know
- Form on every strength move: card on the move sheet, **Form** button on
  the timer (pauses; 5 s count-in on close).
- Three "ease" recovery days running hold any step-up; no test on such a day.
- "After a meal" is a ten-minute standing flow, yours to edit.
- Settings → **Words** is the gym's vocabulary for the app's.
- Offline planner is the floor: a week is never blocked on a network.
- Backup is yours to save; sync is a mirror, not a backup.
