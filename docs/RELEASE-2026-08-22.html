What the app does now, and what changed

Plain words, no jargon. The first half is how it works today; the second half is what is different from the version on your phone this morning.

How it works In plain terms

Your week

Five workouts: three of sets (the muscle-building ones), one on the minute (short hard sets with the heavy bells), one of intervals (the sweaty one, for your heart). Each starts with a few gentle warm-up moves, then one kettlebell or beam standing up, then you go down to the mat once and finish there. You never fetch a third thing.

The coach

One written brief is the coach's entire opinion — read by the thing that writes your week, the thing that checks a move you add, and the chat. Change the brief, change the coach. It only ever suggests. Anything it wants to change shows up as a card you tap or ignore.

Ask the coach

A line under the headline on Today. It knows your week and your numbers. Ask about a load, a sore back, a move. About a cent a message.

Going heavier

When every set of a move reaches 12 reps, two workouts running, the app offers the next weight. It never changes it for you. Big jumps come with a "harder at this weight first" step. The 35 lb bell is for the deadlift only, and only once the 18 earns it.

Where you are

A short baseline — six moves, one set each — every four weeks, and a two-move weekly check after a workout. They count as extras, never as one of your sessions, and they set your working weights.

Form

Every strength move has five lines: set up, the movement, what you should feel, what goes wrong, when to stop. On the move's page and a Form button on the timer, which pauses the clock and counts you back in.

What counts

A mark is a finished planned session. Your own routines, extras and tests are kept as volume and earn no mark. Finished sessions never change afterwards.

Your rules

A move you rule out stays out, variants included. Your saved routines are never touched. No food, no fasting, no medical advice. Sharp pain, joint pain, or pain still there next morning ends the set — burning muscle does not.
